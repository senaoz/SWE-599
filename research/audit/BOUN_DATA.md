## Source

- OpenAlex, filter `authorships.institutions.lineage:i4405392, publication_year:2020-`, all work types: **8,287** records (`works_openalex.jsonl.gz`, raw, CC0).
- List-endpoint records stop at 100 authorships. For every such record the full author list was fetched with a single-work call and only the BOUN authorships were kept.
- Missing abstracts were looked up by DOI in Crossref and Semantic Scholar (free tiers). Their text is **not** in the repository (publisher copyright; the repository is public): the table records only `abstract_source`, and the text stays in the gitignored cache `research/data/v2/boun/`.
- The app still seeds from `research/data/cleaned/boun.csv`, which is unchanged.
